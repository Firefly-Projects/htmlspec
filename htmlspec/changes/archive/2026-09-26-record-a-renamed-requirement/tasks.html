1. Confirm the premises before building on them

  • 1.1 Confirm that a renamed requirement already keeps its identifier: build a two-change fixture that renames req-x with the same id, and check that htmlspec validate accepts both documents and that htmlspec sync --change reports req-x as reached.

    That report is the defect this change fixes, so record it as the "before" state

  • 1.2 Confirm how the projection derives a requirement's title when the heading carries <span class="hs-id">, and whether whitespace normalization makes a reflowed heading compare equal.

    Check with a heading passed through htmlspec format, and record the answer, because decisions 3 and 4 depend on it

  • 1.3 Re-read openspec 1.13.1's dist/core/specs-apply.js rename handling and list each rule against its counterpart in specs/document-schema/spec.md and design decision 1.

    Any OpenSpec rule with no counterpart is either added here or named as a deliberate difference in the design
