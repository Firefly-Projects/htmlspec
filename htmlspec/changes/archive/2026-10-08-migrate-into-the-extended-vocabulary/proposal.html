Why

The nine changes before this one give the destination the vocabulary this repository's migration had to work around, and nothing yet tells a migration to use it. project-migration's "A source distinction the destination cannot express is recorded as open, not mapped to the nearest state" forbids a migration to add a value, so the migration staged in ~/projects/html-spec-migration/ worked around every gap it met. evidence/the-measurement-2026-09-30.md records each workaround and reproduces the counts behind it:

  • Plain links instead of references. The drafting brief says "Never give a unit data-refs naming a unit in another document", so 54 task units and 2 decision units link to a unit in another document, and none of those links is checked.
  • A note instead of a deviation. 76 task units carry a migration note, and one reader's classification finds 33 of them done otherwise than the task's words say. Each is done, and every count reads it as done as written. The brief's rule for tasks and its convention 2 also contradict each other on a struck-through task.
  • Deleted or overstated checks. Review removed two verifications because each record reached only part of its scenario, and two more are staged as passed while their method paragraphs say what the record lacks. The brief offered four outcomes.
  • Summaries that narrate a word. 33 of the 34 modified requirements changed their statement text, and no unit says which words. Five summaries narrate it instead, as in "Its only change … is sync added".
  • A predecessor read around the survey. All 15 .openspec.yaml files cross byte-identical into evidence/, because "The destination has no field for a start date". The survey reads none of their values, and the stager opens each file again for follows:. The files keep crossing, since that is where the creation date lives, but the reason is now the project's: it decided on 2026-10-01 not to record a creation date, because no command depends on one.
  • Risks kept as prose. An accepted risk could not be a unit without a mitigation, so five headed accepted risks stayed prose, and the one drafted risk unit reads as mitigated by default, against its author's "Accepted".

The product side has the same gaps. crates/htmlspec-assets/instructions/migrate.md has no row for ## MODIFIED Requirements, ## REMOVED Requirements, a task's checkbox, or .openspec.yaml, and none of the new words appears in it. migrate survey reports each change's name, whether it is archived, its version, and its delta sections, and nothing from its configuration.

This change depends on the vocabulary each earlier change lands. reject-change-markup-inside-a-unit rejects ins and del inside a unit, as schema/change-markup. state-a-modified-requirement-whole makes a change's specification state a modified requirement whole, each scenario carrying its accepted identifier. see-a-modification-the-merge-did-not-take compares a modification's text in sync and archival, through untakenContent and untakenRemovals. A migrated archive is never synced, so this change asks nothing of that comparison. resolve-a-reference-across-its-change lets data-refs reach a change's specification and every accepted specification, and places an archived change as an active one. mark-the-words-a-modification-changed permits ins and del in a requirement or scenario marked modified in a change's specification, and projects deleted text into deletions. show-what-a-modification-changed adds htmlspec show <change> --diff, and refuses an archived change, because the text an archived change modified is recorded only in git. So it serves none of the migration's archived changes, and this change uses it only to check its marks on a rebuilt copy. record-a-task-done-differently adds a data-field="deviation" paragraph on a done task, and schema/deviation-not-done. record-a-partial-verification adds the outcome partial, with data-field="unchecked" paragraphs. record-how-a-risk-was-settled adds a risk's data-response, accepted or mitigated. One more, record-when-a-change-was-created, was withdrawn on 2026-10-01, because no command needs a creation date.

Why now. This repository's migration out of OpenSpec is staged, and all 37 drafts have been reviewed. Promotion has not run. Its plan was reviewed against a destination that had none of this vocabulary. Without this change, the migrated documents carry the workarounds above, or record as open questions distinctions that the destination can now express.

What Changes

  • A distinction the destination can express is mapped, not recorded as open. project-migration's rule is generalized from the rename precedent. It names what now maps and the requirement that maps each one, and it judges this against the contract a migration is staged against. The migration still adds no vocabulary of its own.
  • A modified requirement is stated whole, and the words it changed are marked. Each scenario carries its accepted counterpart's identifier. Where a source MODIFIED block left out a scenario, the block is completed from the source's own merge, read from its version history. A kept scenario is stated unchanged, and a withdrawn one removed. The words each modification changed are marked with ins and del against the text it replaced. That is required of a migration, although it is only permitted to an author. The marks are the agent's to write, and a computed comparison is evidence it reads.
  • A source task keeps the state its record supports. [x] is done and [ ] is todo. [~], or a task struck through, is dropped with the source's reason. A task done another way is done, with a deviation. An abandoned task with no stated reason goes to the plan's review, and migration writes no reason of its own.
  • A unit the author's words name becomes a reference. A task or decision that names a requirement or scenario in its change's specification, or in the accepted specifications, carries data-refs beside its link. A unit the document cannot reach stays a link. References are checked in the staged tree as after promotion.
  • A partial check is recorded as partial, with what it did not check. It is not recorded as passed, and it is not deleted.
  • A risk's stated settlement becomes its response. A headed accepted risk needs no mitigation to be a unit.
  • The survey reads each change's own configuration. For each change, the payload carries whether it has a configuration file, every key the file sets, and its follows: value. It reads no other value, because no stage consumes one.
  • follows: becomes FOLLOWS, as the source wrote it, and is inferred for no change that states none.
  • The creation date stays in carried evidence. It is neither mapped nor recorded as an open question. Every .openspec.yaml still crosses byte-identical into evidence/, and the date crosses inside it, because the project decided not to record a creation date.
  • migrate.md maps each of these. It gains rows for a modified requirement, a removed requirement, a task, and .openspec.yaml, and guidance on each subject, each citing its requirement. A test holds the rows to the schema, as the rename row's test does.
  • This repository's migration follows, in its working folder: the decisions, the stager, the replay, the packets, the brief, and the draft checker. The three pilot packets are re-drafted as proof, and so are three packets holding what the pilots do not.

  • Unchanged:

    • the document contract, so CONTRACT_VERSION stays where record-how-a-risk-was-settled leaves it;
    • promotion's checks and the staging code. Placement already resolves inside a staged tree;
    • the survey's entity kinds, its counts, and the coverage gate;
    • the rename mapping, and evidence crossing unchanged.

Capabilities

New Capabilities

None.

Modified Capabilities

  • project-migration:

    • Modified: "A source distinction the destination cannot express is recorded as open, not mapped to the nearest state". It gains the rule that a distinction the destination expresses is mapped, and the list of what now maps, and it says that a creation date is neither mapped nor recorded as open, because the project decided not to record one. It is modified rather than joined by a new requirement, because a second requirement would state the same rule's other half. The rename requirement's own sentence, "A rename SHALL NOT be recorded as an open question once the destination can express it", was the first instance of the general rule, and it stays as it is.
    • Modified: "A change's delta specifications collapse into that change's one specification". A modified requirement is stated whole, a partial block is completed from the source's merge, and the changed words are marked. This is where the collapse's obligations already live, and each of the three is about what the collapsed specification states.
    • Added: five requirements, one for each fact mapped outside the specification's requirements. They are "A source task is carried in the state its record supports", "A unit the author's words name is referenced wherever the migrated project resolves it", "A check that reached part of what it names is carried as a partial verification", "A source risk's stated settlement is carried as its response", and "A source change's own configuration is surveyed, and its predecessor becomes its declaration". Each of the first four maps one earlier change's vocabulary, so each can be re-based alone if that change's name moves, as two did on 2026-10-01. The last also states that the creation date crosses only inside the carried file. The design's decision 2 gives the reasons against one requirement for all five.
    • Checked and unchanged: "Migration surveys the source before anything is read or written", whose rule on reading the fields a document carries the configuration requirement applies. "The migrated project is assembled in a staging location and promoted in one step", whose scenario "Checks run in the staging location" already requires what references need. "Evidence crosses unchanged", which governs a change's evidence and not its configuration file. "A source rename is carried as the heading the requirement replaced".

No other capability is modified. The vocabulary belongs to the changes that add it, and this change only maps a source onto it.

Impact

  • crates/htmlspec-cli/src/migrate.rs: Change gains the change's configuration: whether there is a file, the keys it sets, and follows:. payload() carries it.
  • crates/htmlspec-cli/src/commands.rs: migrate_survey's human report names the changes that state a predecessor.
  • crates/htmlspec-cli/src/main.rs: the migrate survey help says it reports each change's configuration.

  • Tests:

    • unit tests in migrate.rs for the configuration;
    • crates/htmlspec-cli/tests/migrate.rs: the harness reads the configuration, a test compares it with an independent read of every .openspec.yaml, and a test holds the new migrate.md rows to the published schema;
    • crates/htmlspec-cli/tests/promote.rs: a staged project whose references cross documents is checked as it will be after promotion.
  • crates/htmlspec-assets/instructions/migrate.md: four rows, guidance on each subject, and stage 3 gathering the text each modification replaced. The five installed copies are regenerated.
  • The working folder, outside the repository, with results recorded in evidence/: make_decisions.py, stage_carry.py, replay.py, build_packets.py, brief-draft.md, and check_draft.py.

  • Not in this change, and recorded so it is not assumed to follow:

    • The full re-stage of this repository's migration. It is the self-migration's next step. It must re-draft all 37 assignments under the revised brief, and it must also take in the ten archives this set of changes adds to openspec/changes/archive/, which the reviewed plan predates.
    • A program check that a migration's marks are complete, or that a staged FOLLOWS agrees with the survey. Both remain the review's and the draft checker's.
    • Rows in migrate.md for a converted document, a release inventory, and the adoption marker. They carry no vocabulary.
    • Re-auditing the staged migration's 90 other passed verifications, or the 8 declined checks that reached something other than their scenario.
    • The 88 risk entries that have no heading. A risk's response does not give them the shape of a unit.
    • The open questions queue.md lists for the contributor, none of which this vocabulary settles.
