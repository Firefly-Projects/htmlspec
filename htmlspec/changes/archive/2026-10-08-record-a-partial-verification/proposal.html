Record a partial verification
Migrated from

Why

A verification cannot record a check that covered part of what it names. Its outcome is one of passed, failed, blocked, or not-run, and none of them means "this ran, reached part of the scenario, and that part held". A verification names units, and the smallest unit it can name is a scenario, so it cannot narrow itself to the clause its check reached. Its author has two choices, and both are wrong:

  • passed, which claims the clauses the check never reached;
  • no verification at all, which loses the record of the clause it did reach.

evidence/the-measurement-2026-09-30.md measured this in the one real case the project has, its own migration, staged in ~/projects/html-spec-migration/:

  • The review removed two units for covering part of their scenario: verify-a-second-store-planted-inside-the-project ("the record lacks 'names both locations'") and verify-mutation-19 ("it checks only the collision check, not what migration produces"). Each check ran, reached one clause of its Then, and found it to hold.
  • Two others were kept as passed although their draft says they "partly cover their THEN": verify-reference-names-a-fabricated-address and verify-a-second-copy-of-one-library. Each method paragraph admits the gap in prose ("The record gives the rule identifier and the exit code"), and the model says passed.
  • The staged tree holds 94 verification units: 92 passed, 1 not-run, 1 blocked. The drafting brief offered the four values and no other.
  • 13 notes, in 9 drafts, decline a unit because the check "only resembles the scenario". On my reading of each against its Then, 5 describe a check that reached part of it, and 8 a check of something else.

Today the contract rejects both halves of a truthful record. data-outcome="partial" fails schema/invalid ("is not one of "passed", "failed" or 2 other candidates"), and a paragraph marked data-field="unchecked" fails schema/projection on any outcome.

Why now. The project's migration out of OpenSpec is staged and waiting. Without this value, the migrated documents carry these checks as passed, drop them, or record the gap as an open question beside them.

What Changes

  • A verification outcome partial. It means the check ran against what the verification names, reached part of it, and found that part to hold. A check whose covered part did not hold is still failed.
  • A partial verification states what it did not check, in one or more paragraphs marked data-field="unchecked", projected into the verification's unchecked field. A partial verification without one is an error, schema/missing-unchecked, the way a dropped task without a reason is schema/missing-reason.
  • unchecked is required on one outcome and accepted on all. A check that failed or was blocked may say what it never reached.
  • A partial verification counts wherever a verification counts. It clears review/unverified-requirement, as failed and not-run already do. It draws review/stale-verification rather than review/stale-reference when its target moves, because a partial check did run. In a run's record it is a recorded outcome, so it clears review/unrecorded-requirement. None of this needs a code branch: every reader of the outcome already takes its values from the schema.
  • The screen stylesheet labels it. The stylesheet writes each outcome as a word, value by value, so partial needs a rule. That makes a new content-addressed revision of htmlspec.css.
  • The contract moves one minor version, because this adds a value and a field. Every document that validates today still validates.
  • The archive instructions say when to use it, and that a check of something nearby is not a partial check of the scenario.
  • Unchanged: the four existing outcomes and what each means; the policy that the review warning asks whether a verification names a requirement, not what it found; what preflight writes into a new run's record, which is passed or not-run; and every existing document.

Capabilities

New Capabilities

None.

Modified Capabilities

  • document-schema: a new requirement, "A partial verification states what it did not check". It sits beside "A task declares completion state" and "A decision records its outcome and rationale", which each state their unit's conditional field. No requirement states anything about the verification unit today, and folding this into "The element contract is published as a machine-readable schema" would bury one unit's rule inside a rule about publication. It does not restate the outcome set. The schema is "the single source of truth for … permitted attribute values", and a prose copy would be a second list to update each time a value is added, which is this change's own edit. The requirement names partial alone, because it attaches an obligation to that value.
  • document-validation: "A specification requirement without verification is reported as incomplete" states that a verification names a requirement whatever its outcome, partial included, and gains two scenarios. It is modified rather than added to, because what clears the warning is that requirement's own rule, and every other word stays.

Impact

  • crates/htmlspec-schema/schema/document.schema.json: partial in the verification outcome's enumeration, a description of the outcome, and an unchecked array property. The schemaVersion enumeration, the $id, CONTRACT_VERSION in src/lib.rs, and CHANGELOG.md move one minor version.
  • crates/htmlspec-schema/doc/projection.md: the verification row of the section 4.0 field table, section 4.8, the normalized-text list in 6.1, the validator rules in 10.1, and the version line.
  • crates/htmlspec-parse: Verification in model.rs gains unchecked. project_verification in project.rs accepts the field.
  • crates/htmlspec-validate/src/rules/relations.rs: MISSING_UNCHECKED and the rule, beside MISSING_REASON. tests/schema_rust_split.rs lists partial as a schema-owned literal.
  • crates/htmlspec-validate/src/rules/review.rs and crates/htmlspec-cli/src/runs.rs: no code change. Each gains a test that pins how partial is treated.
  • Conformance: a valid and an invalid fixture through conformance/src/bin/generate_fixtures.rs, and the rule in known_rules(). Projection: the named-fields and unjudged mapping fixtures.
  • assets/: a new revision of htmlspec.css carrying a partial label. assets/vendor.lock.json moves own to it and keeps the old one in retained. The four templates declare the new contract version, so their pins move too. The specification template lists no outcomes, and its example verification stays not-run.
  • crates/htmlspec-assets/instructions/archive.md: its Verification paragraph, the only place an instruction explains recording a verification. No instruction lists the outcome values. The installed copies are regenerated.

  • Not in this change, and recorded so it is not assumed to follow:

    • Carrying a source's partial checks through migration. That is migrate-into-the-extended-vocabulary, and the four staged units above stay as they are until it lands.
    • Re-auditing the migration's other 90 passed units.
    • A rule rejecting unchecked on a passed verification.
    • runbook-execution and runbook-portability. "A run's conformance to its runbook's contract is reported and never gated" already states that "the obligation is that the run recorded an outcome, not that the outcome was good", and partial is a recorded outcome. runbook-portability's "a verification of each passed machine requirement" is written by preflight, which records a machine check as passed or leaves it not-run, and a machine check has no part to miss.
    • review.rs's has_run counting a value outside the schema as a check that ran. The measurement found this, and it affects only which warning accompanies a schema/invalid error.
    • A repeated method, reason, or mitigation paragraph, which the projection drops without a diagnostic although Paragraphs::single says the caller reports it. The measurement found this, and it is why unchecked is repeatable.
