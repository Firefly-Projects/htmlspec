6. Review

  • 6.1 Resolve what a review of the pull request found, and record what was and was not taken.

    Settle design decision 4, the scenario identifier, explicitly: either keep the sentence, or move it to see-a-modification-the-merge-did-not-take and say so there

    • Taken: holding the whole requirement when a scenario is left out. The contributor accepted sync.md's reading of "neither keeps it nor deletes it": the agent leaves the requirement unmerged until the author answers, because leaving the accepted scenario in place would keep it. sync still reports a held requirement as reached. That gap belongs to see-a-modification-the-merge-did-not-take

    • Taken: the fold-in step is gone. sync.md "Merging one requirement", step 3, told an agent to decide whether an absent requirement was "an addition to one already accepted" and fold it in. That left the added identifier absent for good, against document-schema's "Semantic units declare a change state", which gives added one meaning: a requirement the accepted specifications do not hold. Step 3 now tells the agent to report an added requirement that restates an accepted one, naming both identifiers, and to leave the fix to the change's specification. Step 4 writes a requirement under its own identifier. "Finish by asking again" and "What to report" say the same. no_workflow_tells_an_agent_to_fold_an_added_requirement_into_another fails on the committed sync.md, and also fails when step 3 stops naming the requirement. The five installed sync copies were regenerated

    • Taken, from the code review of PR #41: archive.md let a change be archived while a modified requirement was held. Step 5 sent the agent to sync's procedure, which holds a requirement unmerged in two cases, and step 6 then ran htmlspec archive with nothing between them. Archival compares identifiers, and a held requirement's identifier is already accepted, so it archives without asking and the change's edit never reaches the contract. Step 5 now ends "Do not proceed to step 6 while a modified requirement is held", step 6 says archival cannot read a modified requirement's text, and sync.md's closing paragraph says not to archive while one is held. no_workflow_archives_a_change_while_a_modified_requirement_is_held pins both stops, and that archive's comes before step 6

    • Taken, from the same review: archive.md's report said "Name each scenario you reported rather than merged". That describes a merge that skips one scenario and takes the rest, which sync stopped doing when its step 4 came to hold the whole requirement. The report now names each held requirement and the scenario that held it, and says the agent stopped before archiving

    • Settled: design decision 4 stays in this change. The contributor kept the sentence that a scenario the change carries forward, alters, or withdraws carries its accepted counterpart's identifier. The instructions this change writes already depend on it: sync.md's "Merging a modified requirement" matches each stated scenario to its accepted counterpart by identifier, and archive.md's example gives each scenario its accepted identifier. Moving the sentence to see-a-modification-the-merge-did-not-take would leave both resting on a rule the contract does not yet state. That change's delta already names scenarios by the identifier they carry, so it needs no edit
