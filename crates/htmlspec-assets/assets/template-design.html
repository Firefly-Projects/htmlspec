This page is deliberately empty below the masthead. A design turns a proposal's argument into architecture and technical reasoning, and architectural reasoning is continuous: the connections between considerations carry as much as the considerations do. Headings offered here would be this project's guess at the shape of your reasoning, and a wrong guess is worse than none.

So the structure is yours. Prose alone conforms. Sections, figures, diagrams, charts, and tables are all available, and so are the semantic units — a design that records its decisions and its risks as units is valid, and so is one that reasons the same ground in prose and marks nothing. Which of those to use is your judgment rather than the tool's.

What the tool does still require is that the document works: markup that parses unambiguously, references that resolve, and no remote or executable content. Freedom of structure is not freedom to ship a page that will not open offline.
